Quick Guide to Becoming Great

Daniel Vishnevsky

This might not be anything new to you but I still wish to share it as an increasingly large amount of people somehow miss this. While the guide for achieving greatness in any hobby, whether it is playing the piano, tennis or software engineering, is simple, the execution can be very difficult. It depends on some factors. But worry not, if you find yourself enjoying the process, you'll find it super easy.

What do you have to do? Just do your hobby, whatever it might be. For a really long time. And don't be afraid of mistakes, they will only boost you and make you even better at it. A common trap is perfectionism, don't fall for it! Nothing in this world is nor can be perfect, so you'll only be chasing impossible dreams, which will probably make you miserable. That also goes against making mistakes, which is absolutely vital.

If you find yourself enjoying the process then you're basically there. One thing I want to warn against is getting someone else to do the work for you, most commonly, AI. You will not benefit of any work that isn't done by you, so what's the point of it? You'll also miss out on all of the mistakes along the way.

What should you do if you're starting out and genuinely don't get anything, no matter what? Just keep doing it until you get at least something. Just think of the feeling of finally understanding something about it. It will be amazing! And don't be afraid to seek help. Here's a summary list you can draw in your mind or your notebook:

  1. Do it for a long time
  2. Make mistakes
  3. Don't chase perfection
  4. Enjoy the process

After some time you'll get this moment of revelation that you've really improved over the months/years. For me, it first came after a year of programming games. It felt like I was finally able to create anything given enough time. But don't chase this moment, enjoy the process and it'll come by itself. And, most importantly, have fun!
