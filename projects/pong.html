About

Like for many others, this was my first graphical game ever. Now I'm a proud owner of 3 pong clones - one written in SFML, another in CX and the third one in HTML canvas right above.

First one was crude and only had local PvP. But in January 2025 after having done enormous amount of work on my CX library, I decided to put it to test and remade Pong in it. I liked the process so much that I eventually added new features like AI and 2v2.

The HTML Canvas Pong was made in November 2026 (quite some time later) in like an hour to make my website more interesting. It can be comparable to my first Pong.

Features

You can play against your friend or against AI. AI has 4 different difficulties, the hardest being practically unbeatable. There's also a hidden 2v2 game mode. Source code: SFML Pong, CX Pong, HTML Pong.
