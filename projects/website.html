My personal portfolio and blog website. I publish all of my work here and update it frequently. This page will contain the history of this website. And some old screenshots.

Development

Like most of my projects, this one came to be due to a "fuck it, let's make this". Well, okay, there were good reasons - I wanted to publish all of my projects, both public and private, and to create my own blog. I had 3 Github accounts (2 inactive) and like a ton of unpublished projects. Later on I also added my stories to it.

I started making this site on January 24th 2026 and documented my journey in Hack Club Flavortown to win some prizes on the side. I started out using HTML/CSS/JS, but tried out Tailwind, JQuery and Typescript as well. I dropped all three dependencies and this bad boy has been pure HTML/CSS/JS ever since.

Somewhere in October 2026 I made vast changes to the website, including a lot more soul in the form of pixel art, different fonts, images and playable demos. And also added comments, that nobody uses anyway. During this time, I published an immense amount of private projects - like 20. And this meant adding more project pages.

I update this website frequently and publish blogs whenever I feel like it. If you like my craft or just want to talk, feel free to contact me by e-mail (in the footer). Source code of this website is available on Github.
