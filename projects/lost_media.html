Lost Media

Daniel Vishnevsky

Before I start I'd like to say that whatever I write here may not be truthful as I have no way of verifying this information. All of these projects are lost media to be never seen again. I have no screenshots available prior to November 2024.

In December 29th 2020 I created my Roblox account with the dream of making my own games. For the next few years I would make various games in different genres. Two of my biggest games were a tower defense and an FPS FFA game. I found a kit for both of them and then slowly learnt the code and changed it to my own liking. In the end I made vast changes and turned those two kits into my own games. Only proof of existance is this Youtube video I barely was able to find but that's a pretty rough and old version. I also had videos from a different person about my tower defense game but that channel seems to be wiped of all videos. But eventually I moved on from Roblox and took these games down. Later on I discarded my E-mail associated with the account, permanently making them unavailable to anyone.

That happened somewhere in April or May of 2024. Just a month later I was working on my next game - 2D coin-collecting game made in Javascript. This was without HTML Canvas as I was using knowledge learnt in school. It was a really simple game with multiple levels, something like the World's Hardest Game but without any moving elements. I also wanted to create a level editor (since I was hand-placing all walls and coins in HTML and CSS stylesheets) but that proved to be too hard for me and I quit the project altogether. I don't remember the reason of discarding but I don't have it anymore. I don't remember the name either.

I took a break from coding and in the end of August 2024 decided to learn C++. Somewhere between then and the creation of Abyss Descendant I made a few tiny projects - tic tac toe, battleships, checkers and maybe a few more. In October of the same year I made a local banking system using SQLite. All of those projects are also gone.

It has been a lesson to me to keep all of my projects or at least publish them online to preserve them for later. I have lost almost 4 years of projects and that's really sad. I really wish I was able to show something but I really have nothing to show except for that one Youtube video.
