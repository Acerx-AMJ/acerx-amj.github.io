Lost Media

Daniel Vishnevsky

2026-07-12

Before I start I'd like to say that whatever I write here may not be truthful as I have no way of verifying this information. All of these projects are lost media to be never seen again. I have no screenshots available prior to November 2024.

In December 29th 2020 I created my Roblox account with the dream of making my own games. For the next few years I would make various games in different genres. Two of my biggest games were a tower defense and an FPS FFA game. I found a kit for both of them and then slowly learned the code and changed it to my own liking. In the end I made vast changes and turned those two kits into my own games. Only proof of existance is this Youtube video I barely was able to find but that's a pretty rough and old version. I also had videos from a different person about my tower defense game but that channel seems to be wiped of all videos. But eventually I moved on from Roblox and took these games down. Later on I discarded my E-mail associated with the account, permanently making them unavailable to anyone.

That happened somewhere in April or May of 2024. Just a month later I was working on my next game - 2D coin-collecting game made in Javascript. This was without HTML Canvas as I was using knowledge learned in school. It was a really simple game with multiple levels, something like the World's Hardest Game but without any moving elements. I also wanted to create a level editor (since I was hand-placing all walls and coins in HTML and CSS stylesheets) but that proved to be too hard for me and I quit the project altogether. I don't remember the reason of discarding but I don't have it anymore. I don't remember the name either.

I took a break from coding and in the end of August 2024 decided to learn C++. Somewhere between then and the creation of Abyss Descendant I made a few starter projects - tic tac toe, battleships, checkers and maybe a few others. In October of the same year I made a local banking simulation using SQLite. All of those projects are gone.

It has been a lesson to me to keep all of my projects or at least publish them online to preserve them for later. I have lost almost 4 years of projects and that's really sad. I really wish I was able to show something but I really have nothing to show except for that one Youtube video.

Update 2026-09-04

I got access to one of my old and lost e-mail address and it had a few videos saved in its Google Drive. I published all of them on my Youtube channel. And later on I logged into the account's Github, retrieving 11 private repositories, including the local banking simulation and the source code for the Kahnaroc's website. Rest of the projects I've kept private. This means I do not have any lost projects post August 2024 now.

Update 2026-10-31

I had been working on polishing and publishing all of my closed-source for the past 2 weeks. They are now all public and all licensed under MIT License, meaning you are free to do basically whatever you want with them. I've also gathered all projects from my previous two Github accounts. Here's a full list of published projects: chatroom, encryption-util, multiplayer_demo, CX-Util-Lib, basic-shell, pong_cx, pong_sfml, kahnaroc-website, bank-sim, scripting-language, programming-language-C, CLI-notes, 32bit-VM, MEE, MEE-C, fruitroll, filet-util, esolang-interpreter-collection, abyss_ascendant, CX-Lib and astral-surge. In total 21 projects. Note: encryption-util was originally supposed to be a private tool, but due to me publishing all of these projects I decided to publish it as well.

This means all of my projects post August 2024 are open-source and all available on my profile. This does not include my lost C++ starter projects.
