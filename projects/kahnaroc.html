Kahnaroc

Daniel Vishnevsky

Kahnaroc is my first conlang designed to be minimalistic and with few rules. It has unique punctuation, constant pronunciation and its own writing. You need a font that supports Georgian characters to render parts of this page correctly.

And you can watch this ad for it that I'm not entirely proud of anymore.

You can view all of the rules and the whole 2K+ word dictionary over at the website I made for it. The source code for the website is available for free on Github. Feel free to edit and make it your own. I allow it.

Examples

Kahnaroc can be written in both Latin alphabet and in its own writing based from Georgian Mkhedruli script. That's why there are 3 rows. First - Kahnur, second - Kahnaroc, third - English.

დმ ფოინა კრმყმ!
Zad Acounz Envadad!
The aliens are invading!
"რს აინფ მთ ოიპჲს," სჰლვხე ჯვჲ
"Vat Zunac Daz Outzet," Thancadzun Jace
"What a sunny day outside," thought Jake.

Since Kahnaroc doesn't contain a lot of words you have to get a little creative at times. Also note that all nouns are countable and so "sands" works in this case.

ლ ჩგიპჲ; მქ ჩყგნ ყჰნინოჩ; მდ-სქუ-რგჲუ-ჩინ-ცუ-დბ დნმა
An Frutze; Dra Farn Ahnunof; Dza-Traj-Varej-Fun-Taj-Zag Zandz
One apple. Three sheep. Two trillion forty five thousand six grains of sand.

For real examples you can check my Aesop's fables translations here. But note that it may use an older version of Kahnaroc and that the vocabulary and rules have changed since then.

Development

I was planning to learn a new language and somehow got into conlangs. I've tried making my own languages when I was a child (very primitive, using something similar to a Caesar cipher) and the idea sparked again.

Originally, I wrote all rules and dictionary on paper, however that proved to be inefficient. And that's why I made a simple website using just HTML/CSS/JS and hosted it on Neocities (it's still available here).

In the end it didn't turn out entirely how I wanted it to but it was a fun experience. Maybe it'll serve as a stepping stone for a different language in the future. I know I didn't go over a lot here but that's what the original website is for.
