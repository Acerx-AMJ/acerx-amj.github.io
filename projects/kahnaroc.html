ვლყქა

Daniel Vishnevsky

My first conlang! Kahnaroc is a minimalist conlang designed as a lighter and simpler alternative to English with unique punctuation, constant pronunciation and its own writing. You need a font that supports Georgian characters to render parts of this page correctly.

And you can watch this ad for it that I'm not entirely proud of anymore.

You can view all of the rules and the whole 2K+ word dictionary over at the website I made for it. If you want source then you can go and inspect it with right click -> view page source and then clicking on the script tag path or to view my horrible stylesheet right click -> inspect -> style editor. I allow it.

Examples

First up - Kahnaroc can be written in both Latin alphabet and in its own writing based from Georgian Mkhedruli script. That's why there are 3 rows.

დმ ფოინა კრმყმ!
Zad Acounz Envadad!
The aliens are invading!
"რს აინფ მთ ოიპჲს," სჰლვხე ჯვჲ
"Vat Zunac Daz Outzet," Thancadzun Jace
"What a sunny day outside," thought Jake.

Since Kahnaroc doesn't contain a lot of words you have to get a little creative at times. Also note that all nouns are countable and so "sands" works in this case.

ლ ჩგიპჲ; მქ ჩყგნ ყჰნინოჩ; მდ-სქუ-რგჲუ-ჩინ-ცუ-დბ დნმა
An Frutze; Dra Farn Ahnunof; Dza-Traj-Varej-Fun-Taj-Zag Zandz
One apple. Three sheep. Two trillion forty five thousand six grains of sand.

For real examples you can check my Aesop's fables translations here. But note that it may use an older version of Kahnaroc and that the vocabulary and rules have changed since then.

Development

Honestly, I don't remember why I made this. I'm guessing I wanted to learn a new language and then somehow went in the rabbit hole of conlangs, which led me to wanting to create my own. But that's just a guess.

I remember originally defining all rules and dictionary on paper. However, that proved to be inefficient and I put my coding skills to use.

In the end it didn't turn out entirely how I wanted it to but it was a fun experience. Maybe it'll serve as a stepping stone for a different language in the future. I know I didn't go over a lot here but that's what the original website is for.
